Notebook and source context

Earlier states remain visible.

Some trajectories begin in notebook writing or working documents. These authorized public records remain context—not forced into the publication sequence, and not repurposed as a changelog.

Public boundary: private notebook lineage may exist upstream of public work. Reader Continuity exposes only creator-authorized public context.
