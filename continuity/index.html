Connection map / Corpus finding

The work changes form.

The recurring structure is not a subject category. A relation is first encountered or symbolically encoded, then abstracted, bounded, built, and finally tested—or left visibly open.

The corpus behaves as a translation system.Its most developed ideas recur across fiction, field writing, theory, protocol, infrastructure, and audit. The form changes; the underlying problem remains identifiable.

What the map shows

Three claims worth testing.

These are corpus-level interpretations with evidence and limits—not declarations that proximity proves influence or that symbolic recognition proves external fact.

01 / Strongest completed chain

Boundary becomes infrastructure.

The movement from false consent to selective permeability, scoped permission, local authority, and implementation review is already visible across fiction, theory, protocol, and working code.

02 / Function of fiction

Story stages the failure before policy names it.

Lacuna, Pythia, and Diviner create experiential models of erasure, asymmetric sensing, body-first recognition, and failed boundaries. Later works abstract and govern those conditions.

03 / Consequential open gap

Recognition is ahead of validation.

The corpus has a method for moving from symbol to question and a rule against false certainty. It does not yet have the prospective study required to test whether the detected relations survive formal scrutiny.
