01 / Finding

Technical possession is not yet archival continuity.

A person may hold several platform downloads and still lack a stable, intelligible history. Preservation, provenance, comparison, and union logic are what turn exports into an archive.

The newest export contained 608 conversations. The union across three dated source states contained 654.

03 / Reconstruction

Stable identity comes before interpretation.

Titles, visual order, and the current path are representations. Stable identifiers and retained provenance provide the defensible basis for comparison.

  1. Preserve states

    Retain each export as a distinct dated source. Never silently repair one from another.

  2. Normalize structure

    Make comparable fields legible while keeping their source-state provenance.

  3. Union by identity

    Use stable conversation and message identifiers; retain alternate branches.

  4. Analyze after union

    Record absences and conflicts before making longitudinal claims.

preserve source states → construct the union → record gaps → analyze the longitudinal corpus

The method boundary

Source state ≠ timeless history.

A single export can establish what that source state contained. It cannot establish that no earlier material existed, that the newest state is complete, or that a current conversational path preserves every meaningful branch.

04 / Why it matters

The screen can remain continuous while the computational record changes around it.

Tool events, system context, graph relationships, intermediate states, and alternate branches may disappear or move while the visible prose exchange still looks intact.
